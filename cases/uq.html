UQ Classroom

A K-12 live-classroom app built around three core surfaces — 1-on-1 tutoring, animation classes, and group live class — anchored by a persistent “continue learning” dock. A soft, illustrated visual language was built to feel warm without ever drifting into childishness, so the surface carries equally well in front of a six-year-old or a teenager preparing for an exam.
